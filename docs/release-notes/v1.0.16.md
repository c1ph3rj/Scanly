# Scanly v1.0.16

## Filters that stay connected to the page you captured

Scanly v1.0.16 rebuilds document filters so they look like a cleaned-up version of your photo, not a different processed image.

- Color, Shadow Reduce, and Magic keep the captured page recognizable
- Clean, Grayscale, Text Enhance, B&W, and Receipt still produce a scan look without blowing out or going weak
- Filter strength follows the page: already-good photos stay gentle; dim or shadowed pages get more cleanup
- Glare no longer pushes the filter harder
- Picker chips, the live editor preview, and the saved JPEG share the same look
- Existing documents, libraries, and `.scanly` backups remain supported
