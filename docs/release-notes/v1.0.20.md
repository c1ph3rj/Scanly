# Scanly v1.0.20

## 25 languages, Indra OCR, and Recently deleted

Scanly now speaks 25 languages, can read many more scripts with the experimental Indra OCR engine, and keeps deleted items for a few days so you can undo mistakes.

- Use Scanly in 25 languages, or follow your phone's language; switching updates the screen instantly
- Indra OCR (experimental): a second on-device text engine for Indian scripts, Cyrillic, Greek, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and more, with ML Kit as fallback
- Text recognition has its own Settings page, and existing pages can be re-read with the other engine
- Recently deleted with Undo: pages kept for 2 days, documents and folders for 7 days
- New tools: Add PDF to library, Image to text, and My signatures
- Smarter search: filters and sort, word suggestions, `-word` and `OR`, English spellings that find Indic text, and matches despite typos or OCR misreads
- A new first-run introduction, and layouts built for tablets, foldables and landscape
- Import now opens your photo gallery; the QR tool also reads barcodes
- Smaller app download, and your scans are no longer copied to Google Drive backup
- Many fixes for signing, PDF tools, camera, import, cropping and Indic search
