# Scanly v1.0.18

## Find text in scans, sign documents, and meet Pip

Scanly now reads page text on-device, makes it searchable, lets you sign PDFs or scanned pages, and opens PDFs straight from other apps.

- Search scanned pages by their text, browse extracted text, and correct recognition when needed
- Sign PDFs with signatures, initials, dates, text, and checkboxes
- Sign scans in place; marks stay editable through later page edits
- Draw reusable signatures or extract ink from a photo
- Meet Pip: friendly empty screens, camera coaching, and a daily tip (all optional)
- New PDF to images tool, and Open in Scanly for PDFs from Files, mail, and chat apps
- The PDF reader's menu opens the current file in any PDF tool
- Redesigned camera and camera permission screen, a camera step in onboarding, and new document covers
- Smoother Home loading, and cover thumbnails when adding documents to a folder
- Help & FAQ and storage settings refreshed; library backup and restore removed
- Fixes for legal-page contrast, back navigation in PDF tools, and the Settings rating card
