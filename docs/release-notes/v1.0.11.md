## ✨ What's new

- ✂️ **Richer page editing** — a dedicated crop screen with AI Detect, four-point handles, rotation, reset, and done actions.
- 🎨 **Full-screen Filters & Adjust** — see a large live preview, apply filters to all pages, and fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, and sharpness per page.
- 📱 **Home-screen actions** — add Scan, QR, and multi-action widgets, plus launcher shortcuts for Scan, Import, QR, and Library.
- 🧹 **Library cleanup** — remove empty documents and folders directly from Settings.
- 🤖 **More reliable document detection** — tighter oversize overlays, stronger page-edge selection, and improved handling of desks and keyboards.
- 🖥️ **Large-screen editor layouts** — Crop, Filters, Adjust, and the editor share a polished two-pane experience on tablets and wide windows.

## 🛠️ Fixes and polish

- ✅ The editor preview now shows the perspective-cropped page with its selected filter and adjustments.
- 🧭 The editor toolbar is clearer: **Crop · Filters · Adjust · Retake · Delete**.
- 🔄 Filter adjustments are persisted in Room and included in `.scanly` backups, while older backups continue to restore safely with default adjustments.
- 📐 Filter pickers and editor controls now adapt better to portrait, landscape, tablets, and wide windows.

> 📦 Scanly v1.0.11 keeps your documents local and adds more control to the editing workflow without changing the offline-first experience.
