# Scanly v1.0.21

## A welcome screen in your language, split PDFs, and smoother scanning

Choose Scanly's language before anything else, cut PDFs and scanned documents into parts, preview a PDF without leaving a tool, and get steadier auto-capture.

- A new welcome screen shows the Scanly logo and lets you pick from 25 languages, or keep your phone's language
- Split PDF: cut a PDF by custom ranges, every N pages, or by extracting the pages you pick; the original is never changed
- Split a scanned document into new documents, with page text and signatures copied
- Preview a PDF full screen from Split, Compress, Password, PDF to JPG, Add to library and Merge
- Compress, Merge, Split, Password, Watermark and Save as images open from a document's ⋮ menu
- Screens glide in and out smoothly, and lists and documents appear ready instead of flashing placeholders
- Auto-capture no longer saves the same page twice, and the outline is steadier and tighter, including for ID cards
- New Capture, Find and Finish introduction artwork, and illustrated Tools tiles
- Send feedback from Settings, with up to 5 images attached
- Clearer, more accurate wording in all 24 translated languages
